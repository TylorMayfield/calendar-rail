# Calendar Rail

Free browser extension for Chrome and macOS Safari. Users open Calendar from Gmail's app rail below Meet. They may have several signed-in accounts. Primary design authority is the user's supplied Gmail screenshot. Read and Operate modes. One action, no backend, no payments, no analytics. The active account address may be used transiently for routing, but message and calendar content must never be read, stored or transmitted. Store publication and a public repository are requested; new agreements and purchases require explicit approval.
