Calendar Rail

Calendar shortcut for Gmail

In Gmail, select Calendar in the left navigation to expand Gmail’s built-in Calendar companion across the center workspace. Select Mail, Chat or Meet to return.

Page permission

Your browser asks to read and change data on mail.google.com. This lets Calendar Rail add its button, activate Gmail’s existing Calendar control and resize that panel.

It does not read messages, attachments, contacts, account addresses or calendar events. It has no backend, analytics, ads, subscriptions, account connection or data storage. Gmail handles Calendar sign-in and account selection.

Views and compatibility

This uses Gmail’s Calendar companion, not the full standalone Calendar website. Its available views and features are controlled by Google. Gmail’s right side panel must be shown. Your organization may restrict Calendar.

Missing shortcut?

Refresh Gmail after installing or updating. Show Gmail’s right side panel. In Safari, enable Calendar Rail in Settings → Extensions and allow mail.google.com. If the layout is not recognized, the shortcut stays hidden.
