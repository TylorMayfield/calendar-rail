Calendar Rail

Calendar shortcut for Gmail

Open Gmail and select Calendar in the left navigation to open Google Calendar in a new tab.

Page permission

Your browser asks to read and change data on mail.google.com. This lets Calendar Rail add the shortcut and read the active account’s email address from the account button to select the matching Calendar account.

It does not read messages, attachments, contacts or calendar events. It has no backend, analytics, ads, subscriptions, account connection or data storage.

Account selection

The shortcut uses the active account’s email address, never a saved account number. If it cannot identify one account, Google’s account chooser opens. Google may ask you to sign in or your organization may restrict Calendar.

Missing shortcut?

Refresh Gmail after installing or updating. In Safari, enable Calendar Rail in Settings → Extensions and allow access to mail.google.com. If Gmail’s layout is not recognized, the shortcut stays hidden. Gmail still works normally.
